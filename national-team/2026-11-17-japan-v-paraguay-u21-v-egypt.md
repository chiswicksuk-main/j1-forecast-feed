Gekisaka: the double pivot and back four stayed fixed throughout, so 「6人はパラグアイ戦で先発することが決定的だ」 ("those six are all but certain to start"). Sano Kaishu and Sugawara, who had the most minutes against Brazil among this group, left before the closing 6 v 6.
